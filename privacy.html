Privacy Policy

Ollie Publisher · last updated 25 August 2026

Summary: Ollie Publisher is a personal tool used by one person to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It collects no personal information from anyone, has no other users, and shares no data with third parties.

Who this applies to

Ollie Publisher is single-user software operated by its owner on their own computer. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and no one other than the owner can authorize or use it.

Information collected

None from users. The tool has no visitors, no accounts, no forms, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

The only credential involved is a Google OAuth token that the owner personally grants to their own account. That token is:

  • stored locally on the owner's computer, restricted to the owner's user account
  • used solely to upload videos to, and read back video status from, that same account's channel
  • never transmitted anywhere except to Google's own APIs
  • never shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party

How Google user data is used

The tool requests YouTube scopes in order to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel
  • read back a video's status to verify it published correctly and is flagged Made for Kids
  • read basic channel statistics so the owner can see how their own videos are doing

This data is used only to operate the tool for its owner. It is not used for advertising, not used to build profiles, not sold, and not transferred to anyone. It is not used to train any AI or machine learning model.

Data retention and deletion

Video files and logs remain on the owner's own computer. The OAuth token can be revoked by the owner at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends the tool's access. Deleting the local credential file has the same effect.

Children's privacy

The videos this tool publishes are made for young children, and every upload is declared Made for Kids in accordance with COPPA and YouTube's requirements. The tool itself collects no data from anyone, children included. It has no audience, no interface, and no way for any viewer to interact with it.

Third parties

The only external service contacted is Google (the YouTube Data and Analytics APIs). Use of the YouTube API is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. No other third party receives any data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
