Ollie Publisher

A small personal tool that uploads original children's videos to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What this is

Ollie Publisher is a personal, single-user automation. It takes short animated videos that its owner created — an original character named Ollie the Baby Octopus, made for toddlers — and uploads them to that same owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

It is not a product, a service, or an app that other people sign into. There is exactly one user: the person who owns both the software and the channel it posts to.

What it does

  • Renders short educational videos locally (counting, colors, shapes, feelings)
  • Runs automated quality and safety checks on each one before anything is uploaded
  • Marks every upload as Made for Kids, as required by COPPA
  • Uploads only videos its owner has explicitly approved

Use of the YouTube API

This tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to its owner's channel, and reads back each video's status to confirm it was published correctly and flagged as Made for Kids.

It accesses only the account that authorized it. It does not read, collect, or process data belonging to any other person.

Use of the YouTube API is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
